# Trip Vault

A private, offline-first home for everything about a trip: bookings, tickets, passports, payments, the day-by-day plan, expenses, packing and memories. Domestic and international. No account, no server, no tracking. Optionally, share a trip with a partner or group through your own Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted.

**Live:** https://subhadipgithub.github.io/TripVault/ (after you enable Pages, see below)

## What it does

- **Smart import**: drop PDF e-tickets, `.eml`, HTML or pasted SMS (airlines, Booking.com/Agoda, IRCTC, redBus-style bus tickets, ferries, car rentals). Screenshots and photos of tickets are read with on-device OCR (Tesseract, self-hosted, about 7 MB loaded on first use, or ahead of time from Settings). Fields are extracted on-device and you confirm them. Duplicate files and duplicate bookings are flagged.
- **Plan**: a month calendar that opens on the trip start, colours trip days by what they hold (travel, stay, activities, free) and only lets you pick days in the trip; per-day timelines with airport/station buffers, check-ins, time zones and tappable free slots.
- **Vault**: bookings, documents (passport/visa/insurance expiry checked against trip dates), money (paid vs pending, multi-currency, per-trip exchange rates), packing lists (domestic or international) and emergency notes.
- **Ideas, spend by day, duplicate trip**: a wish-list board (Plan → Ideas) that schedules into the plan, a daily spend chart (Money, collapsed), and copy a trip as a template (Trip menu).
- **Trip menu**: the **Trip** button on Today's trip card collects everything you do to a whole trip: settings, share, show pass, copy itinerary, add to calendar, cost exports, backup, Wrapped and the trip book.
- **Coming up**: web check-in opening, leave-for-the-airport, payment due dates and document expiry on Today. *Add to calendar* puts them in your phone's calendar with alarms, so reminders work without a server.
- **Show-at-gate pass**: a full-screen, high-contrast view of the next flight or train (PNR, seat, terminal, travellers, attached ticket image) that keeps the screen awake.
- **Share to Trip Vault**: once installed, share an e-ticket PDF, email or SMS from any app straight into Smart import. Home-screen shortcuts for Log expense, Import, Pass and Search.
- **Export costs**: CSV for Excel/Sheets and a printable expense report with receipt photos, from the Trip menu or the bottom of the Money tab.
- **Map links** on hotels, activities, transfers and departure airports (opens your maps app; nothing is fetched by Trip Vault).
- **Buses, ferries and car rentals** alongside flights, trains and stays, with boarding-point buffers, pick-up/drop-off events and pass view.
- **Fair splits and limits**: mark who paid and who each expense was for; set per-category spending limits that flag in Trip readiness.
- **Emergency card**: an opt-in medical-ID style card (blood group, allergies, emergency contact, insurer) viewable from the lock screen. It is stored unencrypted so it can be shown while locked. Set it up in Settings → Security; per-trip emergency numbers (insurer, hotel, embassy) go in Vault → Documents.
- **Packing sets**: add beach, trek, business, with-kids or cold-weather items to any list, or copy items from another trip.
- **Account button and menu**: the round button at the right of the header is your profile. Until you connect Google it's an offline profile (a name, if you want one, kept on this device) marked "Offline"; once connected it shows whether everything synced (green), is syncing, needs a reconnect (amber) or hit a problem (red). Tap your name at the top of its menu to edit your profile. The menu holds Connect Google / Sync, sharing & devices, Settings (tabs: General with the day planner, Apps, Sync & devices, Backup & storage, Security with the emergency card, and Privacy & data), Privacy & your data, and Help & about.
- **On the ground (Travel kit)**: Today shows the three tools that fit the moment (Cab, Directions, Translate during the trip; Translate, Phrases, Offline map before it) and **More** opens the whole kit. During a trip, one tap opens your cab app (Uber with the destination filled in; Ola, Rapido, Grab, Gojek, Bolt, Careem or Lyft with the address copied, picked by country), directions in Google Maps, Apple Maps, Organic Maps or Waze, Google Translate, or any UPI app to settle up with a fellow traveller. A Places list with a big "show the driver" card (with the address in the local language, if you add it) and a 25-phrase phrasebook in 22 languages (including Filipino, Urdu and 11 Indian languages: Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, plus Sinhala for Sri Lanka) work offline, with a Play button where the device has a voice for the language, and "Get offline map" opens Google Maps or Organic Maps on the destination city to download. Nothing is sent until you tap.
- **Conversation (live voice translation)**: Today → On the ground → More → Talk (or Translate → Conversation, or search "Conversation"). Each person taps their mic and speaks; the line appears as it is heard, is translated, and is read aloud in the other language. Every step prefers the device and falls back to Google only when it must, and each line says which it used. **Hearing:** the browser's on-device recognition → Trip Vault's offline **Speech pack** (Whisper base, ~76 MB, every language except Odia) → online, the browser's cloud recognition (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, with a one-time notice) → typing. **Translating:** a hand-checked phrasebook phrase when the line matches one → the browser's built-in translator → Trip Vault's offline **language packs** (Opus-MT, ~210 MB per language, for Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Arabic, French, Spanish and German both ways and Thai and Japanese into English) or the optional **Full language pack** (NLLB-200 600M, ~890 MB, any pair of all 22 languages incl. Filipino and the Indian languages) → online, a Google Translate button. Packs are downloaded from Hugging Face on request in Settings → Apps (or from the Conversation sheet), run in a Web Worker with transformers.js self-hosted under `vendor/transformers`, and stay in the browser's Cache Storage for offline use. Nothing from the conversation is stored or sent by Trip Vault.
- **Trip defaults**: in Trip settings, **Spend in** sets the currency new expenses and Money's quick-add start in (Auto: the local currency abroad, your home currency otherwise), and **Language** sets what Translate and the phrasebook open in (Auto: from the destination, or the Settings → Apps choice). Both sync with a shared trip and go into backups.
- **Tour and help**: a five-card tour on first launch (skippable, replayable from Help & about in the account menu), and a **?** in the header and in each sheet that explains the page you're on, so the screens themselves stay short.
- **Search everything** (`Ctrl/Cmd+K` or `/`): trips, PNRs, names, notes, memories and the text inside imported PDFs.
- **Your travel crew**: small chibi-style 3D characters (three.js, bundled with the app; it downloads the first time characters are shown, about 720 KB, then works offline) for a solo trip, a couple or a group, him or her for each traveller, guessed from ticket titles (Mr/Ms/Mrs) and IRCTC's M/F column and changeable under *Who's going* in Trip settings. With *Full* animations they walk in at launch; by default (*Calm*) they just live on the Today card and in the Memories journal, cheer when you create a trip, change the crew or save a memory, appear on the cover and last card of your Wrapped (and in its exported images), and brighten the empty Plan, Bookings, Ideas and Packing screens. Each trip gets its own look. *Settings → General → Character animations*: Calm (the default, no intro), Full (with the launch intro) or Off (no characters, and three.js is never downloaded). Reduced-motion devices get a still frame.
- **Share a trip (optional)**: share a trip with a partner or a group through Google Drive. Everyone can add bookings, expenses, packing, ideas, memories, attachments and photos, and changes sync both ways while the app is open. You can also sync your own trips to your Drive (**all trips**, new ones included, or **chosen trips**) and open them on your other phones and laptops: on the device with your trips choose **Add a device** for a one-time code, and type it on the other device (same Google account). Trips you sync on either then appear on both; Google still can't read them. A short note drops from the account button when a sync brings in the others' changes, and entries someone else added carry their coloured initial, and a ring in their colour marks entries they changed last. Everything is encrypted on the phone with the trip code before it reaches Google; the code travels in the invite link. Off until you share or join a trip, and it needs the one-time setup below.
- **Memories**: a photo journal with moods and highlights.
- **Wrapped**: story cards for a trip, a year or all time. Save as PNG, ZIP, share, or print a trip book. "Hide amounts" for safe sharing.
- **Security**: optional passphrase encryption (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-SHA256 600k) of all data at rest, idle auto-lock, encrypted `.tvault` backups, and a Content-Security-Policy that stops injected scripts and blocks background network requests except Google sign-in and Drive (used only for trips you share). Everything shown on screen is escaped, and data from partners and backup files is cleaned before it is stored.

## Set it up (2 minutes)

1. Push this repo to GitHub (branch `main`).
2. Repo **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: GitHub Actions**. The included workflow publishes it.
3. Open the URL on your phone and use *Add to Home Screen* / *Install app*. It then works fully offline.
4. First launch: tap **Explore a sample** to look around, then **Set passphrase** and **Import a booking**.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000` then open http://localhost:8000. There is no build step and no dependencies.

## Privacy model, honestly

- Data lives in your browser's IndexedDB on each device. Unless you connect Google Drive, there is no sync and Trip Vault makes no network requests. Move data with **Settings → Backup & storage → Export**, or **Send by QR** to hand trips to another phone with no internet (an encrypted stream of QR codes read by the other phone's camera, unlocked with a one-time code).
- With a passphrase set, records and file blobs are encrypted before being written. The key is derived from the passphrase and never stored. **A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.**
- It does not protect against malware, malicious browser extensions, or someone using your unlocked device.
- Clearing site data deletes the vault. Take regular backups (the app reminds you).

- **Shared trips** go through the Google Drive of the people sharing them. A trip file (bookings, memories, file details) sits in the owner's Drive, and each attachment or photo sits in the Drive of whoever added it. All of it is gzip + AES-256-GCM encrypted on the phone with a key made from the trip code before upload. Google can see file sizes, timings and who a trip file is shared with, not what's in it. The code travels in the invite link's `#` part, which browsers never send to a server, so treat the link like a key. Attachments are shared as "anyone with the link" so the others can fetch them without extra Google permissions; the link only gives the encrypted bytes. Removing someone stops their access to the trip file and makes a new trip key: the owner sends the new invite link to everyone still in the trip (their app asks for it), and the removed person's old code can't open anything added from then on. They keep what they had already synced. The Google sign-in token is kept in memory and in the tab's session storage, so refreshing the page keeps syncing; closing the tab or app drops it, Google expires it within an hour, and locking the vault, Disconnect and withdrawing consent remove it. When it has expired, Reconnect is one tap on the same account.
- **Members are trusted.** Anyone you share a trip with can change or delete anything in it, on everyone's phone. If that goes wrong, **Shared trip → Versions** brings back an earlier version of the trip file for everyone (Google keeps versions for about 30 days). Data that arrives from members, and from backup or QR files, is checked and cleaned before it is stored: unexpected kinds, dates, time zones, currencies and IDs are rejected, so it can't break the app or inject markup.
- **Your data and the DPDP Act, 2023 (India).** On first launch the tour's last card says what stays on the device; the full privacy notice (in English, with a Hindi version a tap away; the interface itself is English only) is in the account menu under **Privacy & your data**. Nothing goes to Google until you tick two boxes (you agree; you're 18+ or a parent or guardian is setting it up). Consent is logged on the device and can be withdrawn there in one tap, which disconnects Google and stops syncing. **See my data** summarises everything held, **Download a copy** exports it, and **Delete my Google Drive data** removes every file Trip Vault created in your Drive (trips you own, your attachments, your trip keys). Sharing a trip with people asks you to confirm they know. Questions and grievances go to GitHub Issues, with the Data Protection Board of India as the escalation route. These are the notice, consent, withdrawal, access and erasure mechanics; whether a particular deployment complies is a legal question (personal use may be exempt, and the DPDP Rules phase in), so get legal advice before relying on it.
- **What the security policy can and can't do.** It blocks inline scripts and scripts from anywhere except Trip Vault itself and Google's sign-in and Picker files, so text in a ticket or from a partner can't run as code. It does not stop a page from navigating elsewhere; no browser policy can. That is why the real protection is that nothing in the app turns data into code: all text is escaped before it is shown, and the escaping is tested with hostile input in every field.

## Set up Google sharing (once, about 10 minutes)

Whoever hosts this copy of Trip Vault does this once; everyone using it then just taps **Share** or **Join**.

1. Open [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) and create a project, e.g. *Trip Vault*.
2. **APIs & Services → Library**: enable **Google Drive API** and **Google Picker API**.
3. **APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen** (Google Auth Platform): choose **External**, fill in the app name and your email, and add the scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` ("See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). It is a non-sensitive scope, so you can **publish the app** without Google's review; until you do, add each person who will share or join as a **test user**.
4. **Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Web application**. Under *Authorized JavaScript origins* add `https://subhadipgithub.github.io` (your Pages origin) and `http://localhost:8000` for local testing. Copy the **Client ID**.
5. **Credentials → Create credentials → API key**. Restrict it: *Application restrictions → Websites* `https://subhadipgithub.github.io/*` and `http://localhost:8000/*`; *API restrictions* → Google Drive API and Google Picker API. Copy the key.
6. **IAM & Admin → Settings**: copy the **Project number**.
7. In GitHub, open **Settings → Environments → github-pages** and add three environment secrets: `OAUTH_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_API_KEY` and `GOOGLE_PROJECT_NUMBER` (the number, not the project ID). The deploy workflow writes them into `js/sync-config.js` on the live site; if one is missing or malformed, the site deploys with sharing off and the workflow run shows a warning. They are public identifiers that end up in the site's JavaScript; the secrets only keep them out of the repository. **Don't add the OAuth client secret** (nothing uses it; delete it if you created one). To test on `localhost:8000`, fill `js/sync-config.js` temporarily with a localhost-only client and don't commit it.

Then: account button → **Connect Google Drive** (or open a trip → **Edit → Share**), add your partner's Gmail, and send them the invite link. They open it, sign in with that Gmail and pick the shared trip file once.

## Known limits

- Pick your home currency in Settings (or on the welcome screen). Other currencies convert with per-trip rates you enter; the bundled sample trip is priced in INR/MYR.
- Parsers cover common Indian and international formats; always review extracted fields. OCR can confuse look-alike characters (it read a test PNR "K4XW7N" as "KAXW7N"), so PNRs from images are flagged for checking. Scanned PDFs (pages that are only images) are not OCR'd yet.
- Wrapped distance is estimated from flight time (no airport coordinates are bundled).
- Visa and entry rules are not bundled; the app reminds you to check official sources.
- Shared trips sync while Trip Vault is open (on opening, every few minutes, and shortly after a change); after an hour or a restart you tap the account button (or the sync chip) to reconnect Google. If two people edit the same booking between syncs, the later save wins (packing and ideas are part of the trip and merge the same way). Google Workspace accounts whose admin blocks link sharing can't share attachments, though trip data still syncs.
